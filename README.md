# My-Account-Map
If you are working as key account manager, retail, or any other kind of clientele its always important you spend your valuable time with the right people and the right client. This tool helps in categorising the clients into four categories and recommending the strategy to adopt to each client . 
## How to use :
1. Enter the client or partner company name 
2. Rate the enagagement score 
3. Rate the revenue score 
4. Enter the revenue 
5. You can see the clients are assigned to the respective quadrant and on the bottom of the tool you can see the recommended strategy for the client . 
6. Repeat the same to all the client and on the bottom you can find the clients sorted on the basis of recommended strategy .
